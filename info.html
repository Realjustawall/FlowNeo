FlowNeo Guide

A compact control panel for the FlowNeo helper on Google Flow.
01

Open the extension popup and turn on Enable helper.

02

Open Google Flow, or reload an existing Flow tab after changing the helper state.

03

Use the status area in the popup to see whether the helper is active, waiting for configuration, or needs a reload.

FlowNeo is not affiliated with Google. The extension communicates with flow.cfcnode.com for a remote configuration specification used by its existing helper engine. Review the source, permissions, and network destinations before installation.
